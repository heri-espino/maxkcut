## Purpose and scientific distinctions

Explore **Max-Cut / Max-`k`-Cut** using SDP-inspired embeddings, randomized rounding, and **clustering instead of a single random hyperplane**; then improve feasible cuts through signed-graph local search. Inspiration: Rodríguez Fernández (2021), *On the Study of Fitness Landscapes and the Max-Cut Problem* (Chs. 6–7); Rodríguez-Fernández et al. (2020), *Clustering Improves the Goemans–Williamson Approximation*; and Felzenszwalb–Klivans–Paul (2022), *Clustering with Semidefinite Programming and Fixed Point Iteration*. See [research background](notes/background/README.md), [theoretical notes](docs/research.md), and [architecture](docs/architecture.md).

For original weighted, possibly **signed**, graph `G=(V,E,w)`, measure an actual feasible cut by `C(c)=sum_{i<j} w_ij 1[c_i != c_j]`. **Do not** substitute clustering distortion, an EVA continuous objective, or another graph's score. Convex full SDP (SCS/SDPA; separate experimental GPU PSD-ADMM) and **nonconvex rank-restricted EVA / CUDA factors** are different solver families; numerical convergence is **not** proof of global optimum or a certified SDP bound. `k>2` requires the Frieze–Jerrum pairwise constraint; the 71-graph campaign below is **binary Max-Cut**, not general Max-`k`-Cut.

## What the complete comparison actually says

- Methods: original paired K-MeansNM; independent, sample-antipodal, sample-pair, farthest-pair, spherical-pp, mean-perturb, PCA-antipodal, and GW-screen16. **50 matched restart IDs** per method/graph; base seed **2026**; common postprocessing seed streams. Exact seeds/settings: [Experiment021 note](notes/experiments/experiment021-complete-71-gset.md).
- Best of nine initializers beats the original method's best of 50 on **58/71** graphs and ties on 13. This is **unequal-budget portfolio selection**, **not** evidence that one initializer dominates.
- Across graphwise paired **mean** cut differences, most alternatives are slightly worse than the original. GW-screen16 is **+0.059 raw cut units** on average, but spends **16 extra initial cut evaluations** per restart; the effect is descriptive.
- **G11** reached cut **564** on the canonical weighted instance; see [reference-value distinctions](docs/gset-reference-values.md). **G63** still has historical best feasible cut **26,917**; Experiment019/020 best **26,906**. Do not apply G63's `27047` target to any other graph.
- **No new approximation guarantee or all-graph certified optimum** is claimed. Graphs are the units for cross-instance inference; never treat 31,950 correlated restarts as independent graphs or pool raw cut magnitudes across heterogeneous instances.
